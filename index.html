Privacy Policy for Bloom

Last updated: 12 August 2026

The short version. Bloom does not collect, store or transmit any personal information. Your progress stays on your own device. The only companies that receive any data are Google, through the advertising and purchase systems built into Android — and the ads are configured for children.

What Bloom keeps on your device

Bloom saves your game progress in your device's own private storage:

  • which gardens you have finished, and the stars you earned
  • your dewdrops, and gardens opened by watching a video
  • whether you have bought the full game
  • your settings, such as whether music is on

This never leaves your device. We cannot see it. Uninstalling Bloom deletes all of it, and Settings → erase progress clears it at any time.

What Bloom does not do

  • No account, sign-in, name, email address or profile
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera
  • No servers of our own — Bloom makes no network requests except the ones the Google systems below make for themselves

Advertising

Bloom shows short videos you choose to watch, in exchange for opening more gardens or for hints. You are never shown an advert you did not ask for.

These come from Google AdMob. Every request Bloom makes is tagged as directed to children and limited to G-rated content, so Google does not use it for personalised advertising. Google may still process device information such as an advertising identifier in order to serve and measure the advert. Their handling is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Purchases

Bloom offers a one-off purchase that opens every garden. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your card details, address or any payment information — Bloom only records, on your own device, that the purchase was made. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Bloom is made to be suitable for players of any age, including children. It asks for no personal information, has no chat, no player names, no user-created content and no links out to social networks. Advertising is configured as child-directed and G-rated, as described above.

Because Bloom collects nothing, there is nothing about a child for us to hold, share or sell. If you believe a child has somehow provided personal information through this app, please contact us and we will look into it.

Changes to this policy

If Bloom ever starts collecting anything — a leaderboard, for example — this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
